# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format is based
on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/). Versions follow
the title's `contentVersion` in `sce_sys/param.json`.

## [00.001.000] - 2026-10-23

### Added
- A2 Racer II as a PS5 title (`PPSA69428`): the menu, careers and races, with
  sound and the intro video, on the PS5 OpenGL SDK's Compatibility profile.
- The game's files are copied from the player's disc image in `dump/` on the
  first start, with a progress bar.
- The DualSense: analog steering, throttle and brake in the race; the
  touchpad as the menu's mouse; the D-pad and face buttons for the menu.
- The PS5's keyboard for a new player's name.
- The light bar flashes blue and red while a police siren is heard.
